**Scope, plainly.** Issued in direct response to a Mishap Investigation Board finding on an Agency-level high-visibility close call (a crew duty day exceedance), this NID extends NPR 7900.3D's existing passenger-transportation crew duty/rest rules to **all** NASA flight operations and defines "augmented crew" duty limits for the first time (p.1, our paraphrase). It is "applicable to NASA Headquarters and NASA Centers, including Component Facilities, Technical and Service Support Centers, and Commercial Aviation Services (CAS)" (p.1, quoted). Basic crew duty day is capped at 14 hours (extendable to 16 with a documented risk assessment); augmented crew duty day at 18 hours (p.1).
