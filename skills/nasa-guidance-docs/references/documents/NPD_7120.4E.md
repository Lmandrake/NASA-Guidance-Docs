**Scope, plainly.** The routing table for NASA's program/project-management and systems/software-engineering policy family. §1.a, p.1 (quoted in part): this NPD "provides the statement of policy, principles, and responsibilities for program and project management and system and software engineering disciplines at NASA," naming NPR 7120.5 (space flight), NPR 7120.8 (research and technology), NPR 7123.1 (systems engineering), NPR 7120.10 (technical standards), NPD 7120.6 (knowledge management), and NPR 7150.2 (software engineering) as its implementing NPRs, and states it "also consolidates the Agency's crosscutting software policy and responsibilities."

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found in the text (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm|software|automat) — "software" appears only as the name of the policy area it routes to NPR 7150.2, not as a technical clause.

**Connections and traps.**
- §1.b, p.2 (paraphrase): programs/projects follow "a phased life cycle with key decision points (KDPs)" across four processes — formulation, approval, implementation, and evaluation.
- This is the policy citation for "which NPR governs this class of work" — it has no technical requirement of its own; its force flows into the NPRs it names (our reading).
- 2017 vintage: predates NASA's current AI-governance structure (compare NPD 2800.1F in this set) and names no AI-specific implementing document.
