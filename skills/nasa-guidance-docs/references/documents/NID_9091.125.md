**Scope, plainly.** This NID gives NASA's financial requirements for out-grant real-property agreements — cases where NASA is the owner/landlord leasing out its own property, such as Enhanced Use Leases, National Historic Preservation Act leases, and rental quarters (p.2, paraphrase). It "supplements and clarifies" NPR 9090.1, *Partnership Agreements – Financial Requirements*, rather than standing alone (p.2). Chapter 1 sets general rules: a Center CFO-approved Estimated Price Report, full-cost estimation, and fair-market-value pricing of the partner's price (p.2).
