**Scope, plainly.** Interim-directive update to NPR 7120.11A's HMTA implementation requirements, covering HMTA delegation paths and decisional authority (Ch.2), and procedural requirements including risk management/acceptance, human rating, Certification of Flight Readiness, and formal dissents (Ch.3). Its own purpose: to protect "the health and lives of all Agency personnel from hazards associated with aeronautics or human spaceflight missions and associated testing and training" (§P.1). Applicability to JPL is explicitly unresolved in the text: "Applicability of this directive to the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, a Federally Funded Research and Development Center, is being assessed by NASA. This applicability statement will be updated when that assessment is completed" (§P.2.b).

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found in the text (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm|software|automat).

**Connections and traps.**
- Status conflict: the cover's own printed dates (eff. 2025-08-13, exp. 2026-08-13) read as expired; the OPD register's extension note (to 2027-08-13) is the only place the current expiration lives — the same lapsed-cover/extended-register pattern found in NID 7120.148 (see `NID_7120.148.md`).
- §P.2.b's unresolved JPL-applicability statement means this NID's reach to JPL is explicitly undetermined by NASA's own text, not merely silent (our reading).
- This NID is this reference set's current-ish HMTA procedural text (Ch.3's standards derivation, relief adjudication, formal dissent); use NPR 7120.11A for the stable Technical Authority definition (its Appendix A) that this NID does not restate (our reading).
