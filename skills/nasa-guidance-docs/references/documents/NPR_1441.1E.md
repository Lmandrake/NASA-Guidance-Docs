**Scope, plainly.** Agency-wide requirements for the records life cycle — creation, active use, inactive storage, and disposition (Ch.1 and Ch.3) — plus legal holds (Ch.4) and e-mail/cloud/social-media records (Ch.5). It names the responsible roles in Ch.2, p.9 onward, including the CIO, Center CIOs, Records Officer, and "Information System Owners."

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found in the text (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm|software|automat).

**Connections and traps.**
- §2.1, p.9: "The NASA Chief Information Officer (CIO) shall: Have overall responsibility for the NASA RM Program. Ensure that the Agency information technology (IT) project governance process incorporates RM requirements in the design or acquisition of new or significantly revised Electronic Information Systems (EIS) or services."
- §2.5, p.9: "NASA Center CIOs shall: Ensure records management requirements are addressed in the development or acquisition of any new or significantly revised Center specific systems or services."
- Implements NPD 1440.6 (NASA Records Management) in this same batch; the two should be read together, not independently.
- Ch.5's e-mail/cloud/social-media requirements are the clause set most likely to reach an AI system's logs or chat-style outputs, but by inheritance as "electronic records," not by any AI-specific text (our reading).
