**Scope, plainly.** This short (6-page) NID implements the U.S. obligation under Article IX of the 1967 Outer Space Treaty to avoid harmful contamination, applied to robotic and crewed missions to the Earth's Moon, and applies to NASA Headquarters/Centers and to JPL as an FFRDC (our paraphrase, p.2). It assigns most lunar missions NASA Mission Planetary Protection Category I-L, with Category II-L (added biological-inventory requirements) for missions to Permanently Shadowed Regions or Apollo landing/historic sites (p.4). The Office of Planetary Protection evaluates missions; the Mission Directorate Associate Administrator assigns the category with its concurrence.
