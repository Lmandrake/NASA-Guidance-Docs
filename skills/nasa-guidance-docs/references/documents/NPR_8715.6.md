**Scope, plainly.** This NPR defines responsibilities and requirements for planning, implementing, and reviewing orbital-debris mitigation for NASA-sponsored spaceflight activities, following the National Space Policy and the U.S. Government Orbital Debris Mitigation Standard Practices (ODMSP). Applicability is "limited to objects exceeding 130 km altitude at orbital velocity," with JPL applicability "to the extent specified in the NASA/Caltech Prime Contract" (our paraphrase of the Preface, p.3-4). It cancels the prior NPR 8715.6B and notes that conjunction analysis/collision avoidance is now covered separately by NPR 8079.1.

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found in the text (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm|software|automat); a text search of the public PDF returns zero hits for any of these terms. This is a narrowly scoped technical/environmental-protection NPR with no apparent bearing on AI/ML assurance (our reading).

**Connections and traps.**
- Requires an Orbital Debris Assessment Report (ODAR), reviewed by the Orbital Debris Program Office (ODPO) at PDR/CDR and before Operational Readiness Review, and an End of Mission Plan (EOMP) — a review-gate pattern (NPR + referenced NASA-STD-8719.14 + PDR/CDR/ORR checkpoints) that is a useful structural comparison point for how AI-specific requirements might eventually be organized (our reading; not a claim about coverage).
- Relief from requirements runs through the Mission Directorate Associate Administrator to the Chief, Safety and Mission Assurance, with State Department notification required for ODMSP exceptions — a different relief path from the Chapter 3.2 mechanism in NPR 8715.1B.
