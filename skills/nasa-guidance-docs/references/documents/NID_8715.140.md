**Scope, plainly.** This NID sets minimum standards for storing hazardous chemicals at NASA Centers: limits on on-hand quantities, quarterly inspection of storage areas/cabinets, segregation of incompatible chemicals, mandatory Safety Data Sheet access and employee training (p.1-2, our paraphrase), storage cabinet requirements such as labeling and UL/FM listing (p.2-3), and storage area/room requirements including Authority Having Jurisdiction approval and ventilation (p.3-4). It is a pure facilities/industrial-safety document.
