**Scope, plainly.** A two-sided capabilities flyer for NASA's IV&V Program, "established in 1993 as a direct result of recommendations made by the National Research Council (NRC) and the Report of the Presidential Commission on the Space Shuttle Challenger accident." It describes IV&V as "applying rigorous and repeatable engineering methodologies for evaluating the correctness and quality of the software product throughout the software life cycle," collaborating with projects to resolve identified issues, and "providing qualitative assessments and information regarding system and software risk to project and Agency stakeholders throughout the life cycle." On the applicability trigger: "NPR 7150.2 requirements result in IV&V being applied to NASA's highest profile missions which: Are human-rated · Cost several hundred million dollars · Have high priority and complexity," while "[p]rojects outside of these requirements can also contact the IV&V Program for potential support." It also describes a Digital Twin Factory/JSTAR software-testing lab, Mission Protection Services (software and mission cybersecurity assessment), and SMA support services including Code Quality Risk Assessment (CQRA).

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found in the text (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm). The handout's "software," "software code," and "systems" language is about conventionally engineered flight/ground software; it names no AI/ML-specific capability or service (our reading).

**Connections and traps.**
- IV&V's statutory trigger is tied to NPR 7150.2's software-risk/criticality criteria, not to this handout itself — see `NPR_7150.2D.md` for that NPR's own IV&V-triggering clauses.
- This handout is a program marketing/outreach document, not an authoritative statement of IV&V's charter; its own cited origin for IV&V is the NRC and Presidential Commission (Challenger) recommendations, 1993 — not a specific NPR or NPD section in this handout's own text.
- Personal contact details printed on the handout (names, phone numbers, email addresses) are omitted from this page.
