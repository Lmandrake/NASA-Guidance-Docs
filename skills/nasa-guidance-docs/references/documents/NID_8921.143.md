**Scope, plainly.** This NID establishes NASA's policy for medical investigation, handling of remains, and chain of custody when a crewmember dies "at a launch or landing site, in space, on a spacecraft, or other planetary body during a NASA spaceflight activity or mission" (p.1). It complements NPR 8621.1 (mishap/close-call reporting) by delineating medical-specific readiness and response, rather than general mishap investigation (Note 1, p.1). The Chief Health and Medical Officer (CHMO) acts as the Agency's OSHA Designated Agency Safety and Health Officer (DASHO) and medical authority of record, coordinating with foreign medical authorities, the Armed Forces Medical Examiner System, the National Transportation Safety Board, and the FBI (p.2-3).
