**Scope, plainly.** This NPR establishes requirements for auditing, reviewing, and assessing NASA Centers', programs', and projects' compliance with SMA requirements, "to verify and validate life-cycle implementation of the SMA processes and any related safety and mission success requirements through ongoing surveillance of program, project, and contractor processes" (verbatim, p.1, paraphrasing NPD 8700.1 policy). It defines the audit/review mechanisms IFOSA (Institutional, Facility, Operational Safety Audits), QAAR (Quality Audit, Assessment, and Review), Interim Center Assessment, the pre-launch Safety and Mission Success Review (SMSR), and biennial Safety Culture Surveys.

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found for "artificial intelligence," "machine learning," or "autonom*" (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm|software|automat). "Software" appears several times, always as "quality assurance and **software assurance** requirements" alongside hardware — e.g., "it is NASA policy to certify the safety and operational readiness of hazardous or mission critical hardware and software (including flight systems, support equipment, facilities, operations, and ground-based systems)" (verbatim, p.1) and the QAAR definition: "An independent verification that each NASA Center, program, and project is in compliance with the applicable NASA SMA quality assurance and software assurance requirements" (verbatim). This names existing software assurance as a thing QAARs check, not AI/ML specifically (our reading).
