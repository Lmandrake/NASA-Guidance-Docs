**Scope, plainly.** Implements federal Reduction in Force (RIF), Transfer of Function (TOF), and furlough-over-30-days procedures for NASA's civil service workforce, including the Senior Executive Service, applicable to NASA HQ and Centers and all employees except those excluded by law or the Office of Inspector General (p.3–4, paraphrase). The Administrator, unless delegated, retains authority to approve the need for a RIF and all RIF actions (Chapter 1, p.5).
