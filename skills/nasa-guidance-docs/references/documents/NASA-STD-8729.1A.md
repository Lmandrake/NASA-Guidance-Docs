**Scope, plainly.** The standard "specifies technical objectives and related strategies for NASA programs and projects to be used in planning, executing and evaluating Reliability and Maintainability (R&M)" (§1.1.1). It is approved for use by NASA Headquarters and Centers and "may also apply to the Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL)... only to the extent specified or referenced in their contracts, grants, or agreements" (§1.2.1). It does not apply to facility projects except critical technical facilities (§1.2.2). Its defining feature is that it is **objectives-driven, not clause-driven**: §4.1.3 sets four mandatory top-level sub-objectives (design-intent conformance; functional lifetime; "tolerant to faults, failures, and other anomalous internal and external events"; acceptable reliability/maintainability for availability), while the "strategies" for meeting them are stated as "suggested guidance... that can allow tailoring per risk classification" (§4.1.4). Only 10 `shall` clauses appear in the extracted text, concentrated in §4.2.2 and §5 (planning, SMA Technical Authority concurrence at milestone and readiness reviews).

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found in the text (searched: artificial intelligence, machine learning, autonom*, neural, algorithm, software, automat*: zero hits for "artificial intelligence," "machine learning," "autonom*," "credibility"). The nearest reach is sub-objective (c), fault/failure/anomaly tolerance (§4.1.3), which names a property an AI-enabled function's failure behavior would have to satisfy without naming AI (our reading).

**Connections and traps.** Order-of-precedence conflicts between this standard and other requirements documents are resolved by "the responsible Technical Authority" (§2.4) — never read a project's R&M plan as self-adjudicating a conflict. ⚠️ **Pages 19–32 (14 of 52 pages) are graphics-only and carry no extracted text** — they are almost certainly the objectives-to-strategies decomposition tables §4.1.4 refers to, i.e. the operative substance of the standard. No negative claim about R&M strategy content is admissible from this page alone; open the original PDF for anything on those pages.
