**Scope, plainly.** This NID sets NASA Headquarters/Center policy — applicable to JPL as a Federally Funded Research and Development Center [p.3] — governing commercial manufacturing, production, and marketing activities, and the participation of Private Astronauts and U.S. Government (USG) Astronauts in those activities, aboard the USG modules of the International Space Station (ISS) [p.3-p.4]. It explicitly excludes ISS National Laboratory research allocation and NASA's International Partner commitments [p.4] (paraphrase).
