**Scope, plainly.** NASA's companion handbook to NPR 7123.1 (Systems Engineering Processes and Requirements), covering the SE Engine and common technical processes (Ch.2), the program/project life cycle (Ch.3), System Design Processes (Ch.4), Product Realization (Ch.5), and Crosscutting Technical Management including Technical Risk Management (Ch.6, §6.4). §3.11 covers tailoring and customization of NPR 7123.1 requirements, including worked tailoring examples and required approvals.
