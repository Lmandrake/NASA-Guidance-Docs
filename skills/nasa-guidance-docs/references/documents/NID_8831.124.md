**Scope, plainly.** This NID defines NASA policy for facility maintenance [p.2]: assigning each facility a maintenance "Tier" (1-4) from a Mission Relevancy score [p.3, Appendix A], ranking each piece of equipment by an Equipment Asset Criticality Analysis (EACA, 1=Critical to 4=Low, scored by the worst of five categories: safety, environmental, mission, schedule, cost) [p.4, p.9, Appendix B], and requiring civil-servant facility managers to use the GSA FEDSAT tool for Federal Buildings Personnel Training Act compliance [p.5]. Applies to NASA Centers and to JPL as an FFRDC "to the extent specified or referenced in applicable contracts" [p.2].

**Connections and traps.**
- 🔴 Cover-page expiration date (October 6, 2025) is stale relative to the OPD register's current extension (4/6/27); always cite the register for current status.
