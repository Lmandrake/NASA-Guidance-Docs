**Scope, plainly.** Implements Executive Orders 11988 (Floodplain Management) and 11990 (Protection of Wetlands): avoid adverse impacts from occupying or modifying floodplains/wetlands, reduce flood-loss risk, and set internal controls to monitor compliance (p.1, our paraphrase). "Applicable to Federal lands and facilities under the management control of NASA Headquarters and field installations regardless of location" (p.1, quoted). Defines "critical action" as "any activity for which even a slight chance of flooding would be too great, such as storing lunar samples or highly toxic or water reactive materials" (p.2, quoted), evaluated against a 500-year/0.2%-chance "critical action floodplain" rather than the ordinary 100-year/1%-chance base floodplain.

**Connections and traps.**
- 🔴 **Two numbers, two dates, same document**: do not cite this NID by only one of "NRW 1400.163" or "NID 8500.100" without noting the other, and do not quote a single expiration date without naming which source (cover vs. register) it came from.
- Carries a 1979-era internal deadline (floodplain maps due to NASA Headquarters by February 28, 1979, p.3) inherited from the original issuance, alongside the much later extension dates above — a sign of a long-unrevised interim directive (our reading).
- Section 6's formal evaluation procedure (public notice under E.O. 12372, alternatives analysis, published findings statement) is required for any action in or affecting a floodplain unless excepted under §5.h (p.3, p.6–p.9).
