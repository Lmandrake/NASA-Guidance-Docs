**Scope, plainly.** Governs how NASA formulates and executes Information Technology (IT) service lines and projects, as the IT branch of NPD 7120.4's three-way program/project-management routing (space flight → NPR 7120.5; research and technology → NPR 7120.8; IT → this NPR). Its own applicability text: "This NPR applies to service line and project management for all NASA IT service lines and all IT projects within an IT service line or within a non-IT program... IT projects do not include IT incorporated within space flight, space technology, or aeronautics research projects" (§P.2.b, p.4). An enterprise AI capability (a chatbot, a code assistant, an internal ML service) not built into a flight or R&T project would likely be managed under this NPR rather than NPR 7120.5F or NPR 7120.8A (our reading).

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found in the text (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm|software|automat), beyond generic uses of "software" as a deliverable category — e.g. §5.2 Software/Hardware Engineering (p.25) simply requires compliance with NPR 7150.2 for software development. No clause distinguishes AI/ML-built software from conventionally engineered software (our reading).

**Connections and traps.**
- Governance runs through **IT Authority** and **IT Program Authority** (§2.1, p.8), not the Engineering/SMA/HMTA Technical Authority triad used by NPR 7120.5F, 7120.8A and 7120.11A — the word "Technical Authority" does not occur in this NPR. A proposal to route AI assurance through "Technical Authority concurrence" would not reach an IT-governed AI project through this document (our reading).
- Clause sets an AI-as-IT-project would likely inherit: §3.5 IT System Security, §3.7 Privacy, §3.9 Risk Management, §3.11 Accessibility, §3.12 Independent Assessment (pp.14-16).
- Appendix C (Full Compliance Matrices) and Appendix D (gate products) are tables/figures that did not linearize cleanly in our text extraction; do not cite their contents from this page.
