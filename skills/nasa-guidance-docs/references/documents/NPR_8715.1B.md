**Scope, plainly.** This is NASA's Agency-level directive for *institutional* (workplace/occupational) safety and health, as distinct from mission or flight safety. Per P.2 Applicability: "This directive is applicable to NASA Headquarters and NASA Centers, including Component Facilities and Technical and Service Support Centers." It covers fire protection, explosives/pyrotechnics, pressure vessels, lifting equipment, fall protection, aviation, radiation, and facility safety, and sets out roles from the Administrator down to Institutional Safety Discipline Leads, plus a Chapter 3.2 "Request for Relief" mechanism distinct from the waiver/deviation apparatus used elsewhere (our reading).

- Cover page flags **NID 8715.140**, "NASA Hazardous Chemical Storage Requirements," as an associated interim directive to this NPR (see `NID_8715.140.md`); the NID itself was not reviewed for this NPR's page, so any hazardous-chemical-storage clause cited from this NPR may be superseded by the NID.
