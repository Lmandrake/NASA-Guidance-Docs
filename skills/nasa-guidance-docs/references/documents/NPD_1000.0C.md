**Scope, plainly.** NASA's top-level governance handbook: it lays out the Agency's four governance councils (Executive Council, Acquisition Strategy Council, Agency Program Management Council, Mission Support Council), the three independently funded Technical Authorities (Engineering; Safety and Mission Assurance; Health and Medical), and the checks-and-balances mechanisms — including the Formal Dissent Process — that separate programmatic risk acceptance from independent technical sign-off (Ch.3.3-3.5, pp.16, 20, 23-24). Applicable to NASA Centers (paraphrase; the applicability clause's exact text was not captured in our text extraction).

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found in the sections read, which were Chapters 1-3 only (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm|software|automat). Chapter 4 (Strategic Management System) and the attachments were not read closely, so absence is not confirmed for the whole document (our reading).

**Connections and traps.** 
- Names itself as the authority behind NPD 7120.6A's knowledge policy (§1.a(1)) and the Mission Support Council's charter in NPD 1000.3F — the Technical Authority / Formal Dissent language in both traces back here.
- The cover-vs-NODIS expiration conflict (above) means a tool that trusts only the cover PDF can wrongly retire an in-force directive; the register must be able to restore status, not just read it.
