**Scope, plainly.** Agency policy on managing NASA's technical and program/project-management knowledge. §1.a, p.1: "It is NASA policy to: (1) Effectively manage the Agency's technical and program/project management knowledge to cultivate, identify, capture, retain, utilize, and share knowledge in order to continuously improve the performance of NASA in implementing its mission, in accordance with NPD 1000.0, Governance and Strategic Management Handbook."

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found in the text (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm|software|automat).

**Connections and traps.**
- §1.a(1)(a), p.1 names the Lessons Learned Information System (LLIS) as NASA's "principal mechanism for collecting and sharing lessons learned."
- §1.a(1)(b), p.1: NASA policy "promote[s] an environment for the technical workforce that fosters continuous learning and adaptation to emerging technological and governing conditions" — the closest clause among the documents mapped here to a standing obligation to keep governance current as technology (including AI) changes; it does not itself mention AI (our reading).
- §1.a(1)(c), p.1 names APPEL (Academy of Program/Project and Engineering Leadership) Knowledge Services as an Agency resource.
- Subordinates itself to NPD 1000.0 (the Governance and Strategic Management Handbook, also in this set) — see `NPD_1000.0C.md`.
