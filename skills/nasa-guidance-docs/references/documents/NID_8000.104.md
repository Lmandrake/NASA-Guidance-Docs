**Scope, plainly.** Implements a federal office-space-efficiency standard — no more than 150 Usable Square Feet (USF) per person, excluding public-facing spaces — for new or redesigned NASA administrative/office buildings, in support of OMB "Reduce the Footprint" guidance and the "Utilizing Space Efficiently and Improving Technologies Act" (p.1–p.2, our paraphrase). It is "applicable to NASA Headquarters and NASA Centers, including Component Facilities and Technical and Service Support Centers," and reaches JPL "only to the extent specified or referenced in applicable contracts" (p.1, quoted). The standard takes effect for new leases April 21, 2026, and for buildings initiating design on or after that date (p.3–p.4); it is not retroactive and applies only where a modification affects over 50% of a building's area (p.3).
