**Scope, plainly.** The research-and-technology (R&T) branch of NPD 7120.4's three-way routing (space flight → NPR 7120.5; IT → NPR 7120.7; R&T → this NPR), applying "to R&T managed or funded by NASA (excluding all NASA-funded programs, projects, and activities managed under NPR 7120.5... and NPR 7120.7...)" (§P.2.c, p.5). Its life-cycle, Technical Authority, and tailoring provisions apply to R&T work within that scope.

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found in the text (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm|software|automat). The document's modal density is unusually permissive for an in-force NPR.

**Connections and traps.**
- §5.4 Technical Authority (pp.46-48) describes all three TA types together: Engineering TA, SMA TA, and HMTA, each "funded independently of the program/project" (§5.4.3, p.46), delegated from the Administrator through the NASA Associate Administrator (§5.4.2, p.46). Unresolved TA/program-manager disagreements escalate through "successively higher levels of Programmatic Authority and Technical Authority until resolved. Final appeals are made to the Office of the Administrator" (§5.4.7.3, p.48).
- §5.5 Research Practices (p.48) has no counterpart in NPR 7120.5F or NPR 7120.7A — it ties R&T conduct to NPR 1080.1 and NPD 1920.1, Scientific Integrity.
- §5.6 Tailoring (p.48) is this NPR's counterpart to NPR 7120.10B §3.2; relief requests are "deviations" or "waivers" depending on timing (§5.6.2.1).
- R&T projects crossing into flight work transition to NPR 7120.5's regime under the §P.2.b criteria above (p.5); where exactly that boundary falls for a given AI capability is a program-manager/Decision-Authority judgment this NPR does not resolve by itself.
