**Scope, plainly.** Explains two optional governance meetings — Decision Framing Meetings (most cases) and Pre-ASMs (now reserved for complex multi-element or multi-phase acquisitions, e.g. a campaign with multiple mission elements) — that NASA programs may hold ahead of a formal Acquisition Strategy Meeting (ASM) per NAII 1000.2. It covers triggers, outcomes, briefing format, and how materials and decisions are documented and marked (Ch.4). It is background on how NASA's own pre-decisional acquisition governance actually convenes and documents decisions, relevant to any proposal for routing an AI-assurance review through an existing NASA decision venue (our reading).

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found in the text (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm|software|automat).

**Connections and traps.**
- Chapter 4 requires Decision Framing and Pre-ASM materials to be marked Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) with category markings plus "Pre-Decisional," and outcomes signed by the Convening Authority (pp.10-11) — a procedural fact about how NASA's own meeting records are handled, not a restriction on this reference page's content.
- References NPD 1000.5 (NASA's overall acquisition policy) and NAII 1000.2 (ASM guidance) as the governing documents this guide sits beneath; neither full text was reviewed here.
- Four of its five appendices (B-E: summary table, process flowchart, briefing template, CUI cover sheet) extracted as figures/forms with little or no text; their content is not captured here.
