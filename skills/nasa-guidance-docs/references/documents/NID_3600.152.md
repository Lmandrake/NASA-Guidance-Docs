**Scope, plainly.** Covers only "Sponsored DETOs" — NASA civil-service employees who are a spouse, family member, or domestic partner of someone posted overseas on U.S. Government orders — and explicitly does not cover independent remote-work-abroad arrangements (p.2, paraphrase). Center Directors/Officials-in-Charge approve or disapprove requests (Senior Executive Service cases also need Executive Resources Board approval); the employee's organization bears all associated costs (Chapter 2, p.5, paraphrase).
