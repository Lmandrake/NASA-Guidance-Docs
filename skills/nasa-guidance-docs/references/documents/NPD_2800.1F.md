**Scope, plainly.** Agency policy for governing NASA's IT direction, investments, and accountability under the Clinger-Cohen Act and FITARA (§1, p.1). Applicability (§2, p.2): "This NASA Policy Directive (NPD) applies to all NASA IT and information resources as defined by United States Federal Code 40 U.S.C. 11101, including operational technology and mission systems." It is the only document in this set that names a NASA Chief Artificial Intelligence Officer (CAIO) role.

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** §5, p.4: the NASA CIO's IT-innovation responsibility is to "[engage] stakeholders in data management, data standards, interoperability, open innovation, artificial intelligence, and technology infusion in alignment with Agency priorities." §5, p.5: "The NASA Chief Data Officer (CDO)...works closely with the NASA Chief Artificial Intelligence (CAIO) Officer, leads and coordinates OCIO support to the CAIO and serves on the agency artificial intelligence governance." §6, p.7: the NASA CIO "delegates to DCIOs, Center CIOs, CDO, CAIO, SAISO, DTO, ASM, Program Managers, SL Directors, and ALO Chiefs IT Authority and IT Program Authority." Attachment B, p.8 defines the acronym "CAIO - Chief Artificial Intelligence Officer."

**Connections and traps.**
- Names a concrete AI-governance role (CAIO) and ties it to the CDO/OCIO structure, but **does not define the CAIO's charter, authority, or relationship** to NPD 1000.0C's three Technical Authorities — that mapping is not resolved by this document (our reading).
- Cites NPD 1000.3 (NASA Organization), NPR 2810.1 (Security of Information and Information Systems), and NPD 1001.0 (NASA Strategic Plan) as authority/applicable documents (§3-4, p.2).
- §5, p.6: Mission Directorate Associate Administrators must "identify an Agency-level representative for Mission IT matters to coordinate with OCIO," including "monitoring, managing, mitigating, and accepting identified mission-related cybersecurity risks" per NPR 2810.1.
- Supersedes NPD 2800.1E (§8, p.9 Cancellation).
