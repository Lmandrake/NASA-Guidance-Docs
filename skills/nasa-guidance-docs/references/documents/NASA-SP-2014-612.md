**Scope, plainly.** Volume 2 is implementation guidance for Volume 1's RISC concepts (§1 Introduction), covering acquirer and provider roles, setting minimum tolerable safety thresholds and goals with a worked example (Ch.4, using Space Shuttle/launch-vehicle experience), the Provider's job of building a System Safety Management Plan and performing an Integrated Safety Analysis (Ch.5), **integrating software analyses into that safety analysis (§5.2.7)**, developing and documenting the RISC (Ch.6), and the Acquirer's job of reviewing, scoring, and evaluating a submitted RISC (Ch.7, including "sources of assurance deficit" and value-of-information methods).

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found in the text (searched: artificial intelligence, machine learning, autonom*, neural, algorithm, software, automat*). §5.2.7 is the closest reach: it reports that the software-attributable fraction of JPL planetary-mission incident/anomaly reports rose from about 10% (end of 1996) to about 35% (end of 2006), three-quarters of that ground software, and describes NASA's Context-based Software Risk Model (CSRM) for folding software failure events into fault-tree/event-tree hardware probabilistic risk assessment — a software-risk integration method that an AI-enabled function's safety case would need to reach through, even though the text never names AI (our reading). §5.2.9 separately names a Modeling and Simulation Credibility Assessment Scale, which is the handbook's own bridge toward NASA-STD-7009's model-credibility machinery.

**Connections and traps.** Volume 1 (NASA/SP-2010-580) is the prerequisite — Volume 2 presupposes its objectives→claims→arguments→evidence structure and does not re-derive it. §5.2.7's "sources of assurance deficit" (Ch.7) is the handbook's own named vocabulary for where a RISC can be weak; worth checking against any AI-specific assurance-deficit claim before treating it as novel. Chapters 6–7 close with worked examples (an electric-ion-thruster escape-pod case, §6.6/§5.7, and a RISC evaluation tree, §7.5) that are largely carried in figures and tables that did not survive text extraction cleanly — open the original PDF for those.
