**Scope, plainly.** This NID sets Agency requirements for establishing, strategically managing, and terminating "capability portfolios" (CPs) — groups of functionally similar NASA institutional capabilities such as test facilities (e.g., wind tunnels) and their enabling infrastructure — applicable to NASA Centers and, per NOJMO determination, to JPL as the Agency's FFRDC [p.3] (paraphrase). It defines three CP lifecycle stages (Establishment, Strategic Management, Termination) gated by Stage Transition Reviews [p.6-p.9].
