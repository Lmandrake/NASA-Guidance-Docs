**Scope, plainly.** This NPR defines how NASA generates, distributes, assesses, and closes out reports of significant parts/materials/process/software/safety problems — internally via NASA Advisories (through the NASA Advisory Reporting System, NARS) and externally via GIDEP (our paraphrase). It applies to JPL as an FFRDC "only to the extent specified in its contracts/agreements" (our paraphrase, p.4). It cancels the prior NPR 8735.1C.

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found for "artificial intelligence," "machine learning," or "autonom*" (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm|software|automat). "Software" appears as one of several problem-data categories this NPR exchanges information about — e.g., the Purpose states the directive covers problem data on "parts/materials/processes/software/safety" (our paraphrase, P.1-P.2), and Appendix A defines "**Nonconformance**" verbatim as "The state or situation of not fulfilling a requirement. A nonconforming product, process, software, or material does not meet manufacturing specifications or design, composition, or contractual requirements. Counterfeit parts, products, software, and materials are considered nonconforming and/or nonconformances." "Automat*" appears once, in the Appendix A definition of "Payload," which lists "free-flying automated spacecraft" as an example — a generic use, not AI/ML (our reading).

**Connections and traps.**
- Defines the exact triggering criteria for generating a NASA Advisory or GIDEP Notice — counterfeit/suspect-counterfeit items, or a major/critical nonconformance that is commercially available/common or has already escaped to more than one customer (§3.4, our paraphrase, p.11-12) — the closest existing NASA mechanism for reporting and tracking anomalies, nonconformances, and counterfeit items across programs and contractors (our reading; a possible structural analogue for reporting an AI-related failure or capability change, not a claim that it currently covers one).
- Appendix C gives suggested contract text requiring contractors to participate in GIDEP and generate their own notices for discovered nonconforming items (p.17-18) — the one document here that reaches contractor flow-down obligations by name.
- Routes Inspector General fraud/waste/abuse findings into a NASA Advisory with distribution caution (§3.4, p.11-12) — a separate trigger path from the counterfeit/nonconformance one above.
