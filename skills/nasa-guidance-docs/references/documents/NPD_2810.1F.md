**Scope, plainly.** Agency policy for protecting classified and unclassified NASA information and information systems (§1.a, p.1). This is the *policy* parent; the actual mandatory controls live in the companion NPR 2810.1F, "Security of Information and Information Systems," which already has its own long-form page in this skill (`NPR_2810.1F.md`) — do not confuse the two; they share a number but differ in kind (NPD vs. NPR).

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found in the text (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm|software|automat); it reaches an AI system only as an information system, never as AI specifically (our reading).

**Connections and traps.**
- §1.b(2), p.1: NASA policy is to "Fully implement the guidance in National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Special Publication 800 series...and the NIST Federal Information Processing Standards (FIPS) as directed in Office of Management and Budget" (OMB Circular A-130, FISMA 2014) — this is the FISMA/NIST authority hook that NPR 2810.1F implements with concrete controls.
- §1.b(4), p.1 (paraphrase): directs management of cybersecurity for "all classified and unclassified systems acquired, developed, or used in support of NASA missions through the complete system life cycle."
- 🔴 **Numbering trap:** two separate PDFs share "2810.1F" — one an NPD (nine pages, policy only), one an NPR (78 pages, the NIST-CSF-structured requirements). Cite the kind, not just the number.
