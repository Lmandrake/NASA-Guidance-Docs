**Scope, plainly.** This NID sets the process by which NASA verifies that its own medical requirements have been met by "Private Astronauts" (non-government crew sponsored by a commercial entity) and "NASA Sub-Orbital Spaceflight Participants" when they fly on or near NASA vehicles or conduct NASA-sponsored research (p.2, paraphrase). It chains FAA 14 CFR 460 certification by the commercial entity's own physician to an Aerospace Medicine Board (AMB) recommendation, a Chief Health and Medical Officer (CHMO) accept/deny decision, and — for International Space Station (ISS) missions — a Multilateral Space Medicine Board (MSMB) review (Ch.2, Ch.5, p.6, p.12-14).
