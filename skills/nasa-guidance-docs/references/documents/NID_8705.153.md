**Scope, plainly.** This NID rewrites how NASA conducts SMA audits, reviews, and assessments, shifting from centralized audits toward Centers assessing their own SMA conformance (our paraphrase, p.2). It replaces IFOSA and QAAR (NPR 8705.6's terms) with AIO (Assessments and Investigations Office) Assessments, AIO Safety Audits, and Independent Assessments (p.2). Chapter 3 assigns Center Directors responsibility for compliance audits/assessments/reviews, with the Chief, SMA able to run targeted independent assessments, and Safety Culture Surveys on a roughly two-year cycle with 90/120-day reporting windows (p.7). Chapter 4 defines the pre-launch Safety and Mission Success Review (SMSR) / Safety and Mission Success Assessment (SMSA), chaired by NASA's three Technical Authorities — Chief Engineer, Chief SMA, and Chief Health and Medical Officer (p.9-10).
