**Scope, plainly.** This NPR sets the roles, responsibilities, and procedures for preventing harmful biological contamination — both "forward" (Earth-to-other-body) and "backward" (other-body-to-Earth) — on NASA's robotic missions to other solar system bodies, including restricted sample return. It implements U.S. obligations under the Outer Space Treaty and follows COSPAR planetary-protection policy and National Academies advice (our reading). It cancels the prior NPR 8020.12D and two NIDs. Applicability covers all robotic missions, including secondary payloads and sample return, with the lead partner/agency responsible for compliance on partnered missions.

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** No occurrence of "artificial intelligence," "machine learning," or "autonom*" found in a text search of the public PDF (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm|software|automat). "Software" appears only inside a definition and a references entry, both citing the same external standard: Appendix A's "**Assurance Case**" entry paraphrases to a reasoned, auditable body of argument and evidence supporting a top-level claim, and attributes the definition to ISO/IEC/IEEE 15026-1:2019, Systems and software engineering — Systems and software assurance [p.19 of the document]. Appendix D (References), item D.10, cites the same standard. This is NASA directly invoking the systems/software assurance-case standard — a notable structural touchpoint for assurance-case approaches to AI, even though the NPR's own subject (biological contamination) has nothing to do with AI (our reading).

**Connections and traps.**
- Appendix D.6 cites "**NID 8715.129**, Biological Planetary Protection for Human Missions to Mars" as a referenced document — meaning NID 8715.129 (see `NID_8715.129.md`) supplements this NPR's framework for the harder, human-mission case.
- The requirement for a documented "assurance case" (per ISO/IEC/IEEE 15026) applies specifically to restricted sample-return biological containment [p.17-18 of the document] — a narrow, non-AI use of the same assurance-case vocabulary used for AI assurance elsewhere.
