**Scope, plainly.** This NPR governs how NASA reports, investigates, classifies, and documents mishaps and close calls — injury, property damage, or mission failure — implementing the Federal employee occupational safety and health program requirements of 29 CFR pt. 1960 (our paraphrase of the Preface). Chapters cover readiness to investigate, response/notification/classification, selecting an Investigating Authority, the investigation process (fault tree analysis, HFACS), the Mishap Investigation Report, post-investigation Corrective Action Plans and lessons learned, and special provisions for commercial-launch, aircraft, and (reserved) spacecraft mishaps.

**AI and machine-learning touchpoints.** None found for "artificial intelligence," "machine learning," or "autonom*" (searched: artificial intelligence|machine learning|autonom|neural|algorithm|software|automat). "Software" appears a handful of times, all generic rather than AI-specific: in a definitions appendix, "**Control**" is defined as "An active mechanism used to detect the initiating event or the hazard or both, and enable an active device (hardware, software, environmental, or human) to prevent or reduce the likelihood of the hazard affecting a target," and "**Flight Software**" is defined as "Any software that is flown on or part of an aircraft, experimental flight vehicle, satellite, lighter than air vehicles, unoccupied aerial vehicle, or space transportation system" (both verbatim, from the appendix read for `NID_8621.157.md`'s identical body text). "High-Visibility Mishap or Close Call" also names "flight hardware or software" as something a mishap can affect. None of these name AI/ML (our reading).

**Connections and traps.**

- Only roughly the first three-quarters of this 80-page document was reviewed here (through the start of Chapter 7's commercial-launch section); Chapter 8 (aircraft mishaps), Chapter 9, and Appendices A-J were not reviewed. Do not cite classification thresholds, Investigating Authority composition rules, or Corrective Action Plan content requirements from this page without checking the body text directly.
