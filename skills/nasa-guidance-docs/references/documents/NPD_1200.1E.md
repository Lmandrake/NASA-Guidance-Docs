**Scope, plainly.** NASA's enterprise internal-control and risk-management policy, implementing OMB Circular A-123 and the Federal Managers' Financial Integrity Act (§1, p.1). Applicability (§2, p.2): "This NASA Policy Directive (NPD) applies to NASA Headquarters and NASA Centers, including Component Facilities and Service Support Centers. The Jet Propulsion Laboratory, a Federally Funded Research and Development Center, is subject to its own internal control requirements and procedures consistent with its status as a government-owned, contractor-operated facility."

**Connections and traps.**
- §5, p.3-4: Officials-in-Charge and Center Directors "shall" maintain internal control per the GAO's five standard components (control environment, risk assessment, control activities, information/communication, monitoring).
- §5, p.5 names the Chief Information Officer's internal-control duties verbatim: "The Chief Information Officer (CIO) shall: Provide oversight of the management of NASA's information resources as required by OMB Circular A-130. Ensure that NASA protects all NASA information and associated information technology systems...as required by 44 U.S.C. § 3541 et seq. and NPR 2810.1."
- Cites NPR 2810.1 (Security of Information and Information Systems) and NC 1000.8 (NASA Senior Assessment Team Charter) — neither's text is reproduced here.
- JPL is explicitly carved out to its own internal-control regime (§2, p.2) — do not read NPD 1200.1E's "shall" clauses as binding JPL directly.
